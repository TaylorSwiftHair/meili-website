# MeiLi
MeiLi is a beauty and self-care app that aims to create a space where women can learn and start their hair care and makeup journey. Empowering them to feel confident and embracing their true self. When users open the app, they are greeted by a welcome screen with a START button that leads to the main home screen, where they can choose from four sections: AI Hair Type Scanner, Makeup Quiz, Hair Catalog, and Makeup Catalog. The AI Hair Type Scanner uses a camera-based analysis and a database of hair types to estimate a user's hair type according to the Andre Walker Hair Typing System (1A–4C). Users take four photographs of their hair from different angles and complete a short questionnaire about their hair care habits and concerns. By combining their photographs and answers, MeiLi generates an estimated hair type, an assessment of potential hair damage, personalized product recommendations based on their budget, and a recommended hair care routine. The routine features original, hand-drawn illustrations created by a team member in Procreate, guiding users through each step, from showering to post-shower care. The second button is to go to the Makeup Quiz which is a set of 15 questions to give the user an estimate of their recommended makeup routine based on the Makeup Catalog. After the person takes the Makeup questionnaire they will be sent to their recommended makeup look routine. This routine is also hand drawn on Procreate and shows each makeup step with detailed descriptions of what to do. The 3rd button is to go to the Hair Catalog which shows the hand drawn hair routines in a carousel layout for a person to choose from, the 4th button is to go to the Makeup Catalog which shows the hand drawn makeup routines in a carousel layout for a person to choose from. The Catalogs allow users to explore additional illustrated hair care routines and makeup tutorials, giving them the freedom to discover and choose looks that interest them. Ultimately, MeiLi aims to make beauty education more accessible by helping users understand their features and discover new techniques in a fun and approachable way.
